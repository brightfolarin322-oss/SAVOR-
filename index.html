Our Story

Familiar roots.
Unexpected plates.

SAVORÉ was created from a simple belief: African food deserves to be experienced with the same curiosity, creativity and attention to detail as any great cuisine.

We take ingredients and flavors that feel familiar and give them a contemporary point of view — without losing the soul that made them special in the first place.

The result is a dining experience that feels rooted, refined and unmistakably ours.

Meet the Chef

Chef Amara
Okafor.

“Food should remind you of somewhere, even when you've never been there before.”

Chef Amara's cooking is shaped by childhood memories, Nigerian ingredients and a constant curiosity about what food can become.

At SAVORÉ, she brings these influences together with modern technique to create dishes that respect tradition without being limited by it.
